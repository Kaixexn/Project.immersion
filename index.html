Before you continue

Location & data consent

This app records your GPS location only to confirm attendance at your assigned workplace. Read what that means below.

  • Your location is checked when you time in and time out, and periodically while you're checked in.
  • Your name, age, section, and assigned workplace are stored so your teacher can verify attendance.
  • This data is visible to your immersion coordinator/teacher only, and used solely for attendance purposes.
  • You can request your data be removed after the immersion period ends.
